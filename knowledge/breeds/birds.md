# Bird breed guides

## Cockatiel
Social, needing one to three hours out of the cage daily. They produce powder dust, so keep the cage and room clean. A seed-only diet leads to fatty liver disease, so feed pellets and vegetables. Prone to night frights, so use a dim night light.

## Budgerigar (Parakeet)
Small and social, needing one to two hours out of the cage. Seed-only diets cause obesity and tumors, so offer pellets and vegetables. Provide a cuttlebone and toys.

## African Grey Parrot
Highly intelligent, needing daily mental stimulation and two or more hours out of the cage. Prone to calcium deficiency and feather plucking from boredom. Can live 40 to 60 years.

## Canary
Usually kept for their song and not handled. Needs a large cage for flying, fresh bathing water, and no drafts.
