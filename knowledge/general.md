# General pet care

## Giving medication
Give medication at the same times every day, with food if the vet says to. Never skip or double a dose. Set recurring daily tasks for medications so they are not forgotten. Do not stack medications at the same time as other tasks, so there is time to confirm the pet swallowed it.

## Scheduling tips and conflicts
Avoid booking two tasks at the same time. Leave a gap between a vet visit and other tasks for travel. Do not schedule exercise right before or after a meal. Spread tasks across the day so each pet gets individual attention.

## Senior pets
Older pets need more frequent vet visits, gentler exercise and consistent routines. Watch for changes in appetite, weight, mobility and thirst, and tell the vet.

## Emergencies
Call a vet right away for vomiting that continues, trouble breathing, collapse, suspected poisoning or a pet that stops eating or drinking. This app gives general guidance and does not replace veterinary advice.
