# Rabbit breed guides

## Holland Lop
A small lop-eared rabbit. Check the ears regularly for wax and infection and watch for teeth overgrowth. Unlimited hay is essential.

## Netherland Dwarf
Tiny and sometimes skittish, so handle gently. The short face makes dental problems common. Provide unlimited hay to wear teeth down.

## Mini Rex
The short, velvety coat needs gentle brushing. Prone to sore hocks, so provide soft flooring and never wire floors.

## Lionhead
The mane needs weekly brushing, and more during molting, because swallowed fur can cause blockages.

## Flemish Giant
A very large rabbit that needs a lot of space and bigger food portions. Prone to sore hocks and heart problems, with a shorter lifespan of roughly 5 to 8 years.
