# Cat care

## Cat feeding schedule
Adult cats do well with two measured meals a day, or several small meals. Kittens need three to four meals a day. Always provide fresh water, and consider a water fountain because many cats drink more from moving water.

## Cat play and enrichment
Cats benefit from two or three short play sessions of 10 to 15 minutes per day with wand toys or puzzle feeders. Provide scratching posts and climbing spaces. Play before meals mimics natural hunting.

## Cat grooming
Brush short-haired cats weekly and long-haired cats such as Persians daily to prevent mats and hairballs. Trim claws every 2 to 3 weeks. Cats rarely need baths.

## Cat litter box care
Scoop the litter box at least once a day and fully change the litter weekly. Keep one box per cat plus one extra.

## Cat vet visits
Healthy adult cats need a yearly wellness exam. Kittens need vaccines every 3 to 4 weeks until about 16 weeks. Senior cats (10 years and older) should be examined every six months.
