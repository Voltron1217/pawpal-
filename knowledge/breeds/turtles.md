# Turtle and tortoise breed guides

## Red-Eared Slider
Needs a very large tank, roughly 10 gallons per inch of shell, with strong filtration plus basking and UVB light. They grow to 8 to 12 inches and can live 20 to 30 years or more. They can carry salmonella, so wash your hands after handling.

## Russian Tortoise
A land tortoise that eats mostly weeds and leafy greens. Needs UVB light, a basking spot, dry substrate and room to dig. Only hibernate under a vet's guidance.

## Box Turtle
Semi-terrestrial and needs a humid habitat with UVB light. They are omnivores and can live for decades.
