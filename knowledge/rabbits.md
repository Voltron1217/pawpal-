# Rabbit care

## Rabbit feeding
Unlimited timothy or grass hay should make up most of a rabbit's diet. Add a handful of fresh leafy greens daily and a small measured portion of pellets. Treats such as fruit should be occasional. Fresh water must always be available.

## Rabbit exercise and play
Rabbits need at least 3 to 4 hours a day outside their enclosure in a rabbit-proofed area. Provide tunnels, chew toys and cardboard boxes for enrichment.

## Rabbit grooming
Brush weekly, and daily during molting. Trim nails every 4 to 6 weeks. Check teeth, ears and the rear end regularly. Never bathe a rabbit in water.

## Rabbit health and vet visits
Rabbits need a yearly check-up with a rabbit-savvy vet, and spaying or neutering is usually recommended. A rabbit that stops eating or produces no droppings for 12 hours is an emergency, so see a vet immediately.
