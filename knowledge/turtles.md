# Turtle and tortoise care

## Turtle feeding
Young water turtles are fed daily and adults every 2 to 3 days with a mix of pellets, leafy greens and occasional protein. Tortoises eat mostly leafy greens and weeds daily. Remove uneaten food to keep water clean.

## Turtle lighting and habitat
Turtles and tortoises need a basking spot and UVB light for 10 to 12 hours a day. Replace UVB bulbs about every 6 months, even if they still glow. Keep water and basking temperatures steady.

## Turtle tank cleaning
Do a partial water change weekly and clean the filter regularly. Wash your hands with soap after handling any turtle or tortoise, because they can carry salmonella.

## Turtle vet visits
Take reptiles for a yearly check-up with a reptile vet. Swollen eyes, a soft shell or not eating can mean illness and need a vet.
