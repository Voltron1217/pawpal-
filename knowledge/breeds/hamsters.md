# Hamster breed guides

## Syrian (Golden)
Must live alone. Needs a large wheel of at least 8 inches, and is nocturnal, so feed and handle in the evening.

## Roborovski Dwarf
Tiny and fast, and usually does not enjoy handling. Offer a sand bath and a wheel of at least 6 inches.

## Dwarf Campbell's Russian
Prone to diabetes, so avoid sugary treats and fruit. Needs a wheel of at least 6 inches.
