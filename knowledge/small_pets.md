# Hamster and guinea pig care

## Hamster feeding and schedule
Hamsters are nocturnal, so feed them in the evening. Give about one tablespoon of a quality hamster mix with a small piece of fresh vegetable. Remove uneaten fresh food the next day and keep water fresh.

## Hamster exercise and cleaning
Hamsters need a solid-surface wheel of the right size and run at night. Spot clean the cage daily and do a full clean every one to two weeks. Do not bathe hamsters in water. Syrian hamsters must live alone.

## Guinea pig feeding
Guinea pigs need unlimited timothy hay, fresh vegetables and a little pellet food each day. They cannot make vitamin C, so give vitamin C rich vegetables such as bell pepper every day.

## Guinea pig exercise and care
Guinea pigs are social and do best in pairs. Give at least an hour of floor time daily, spot clean the cage daily and fully clean it weekly. Trim nails about every 4 weeks and book a yearly vet check.
