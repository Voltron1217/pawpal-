# Dog care

## Dog walking and exercise
Most adult dogs need 30 to 60 minutes of exercise per day, split into two or more walks. High-energy breeds such as Border Collies, Labradors and Beagles often need 60 to 120 minutes. Puppies need shorter, more frequent outings, and senior dogs benefit from gentle, shorter walks. Avoid hard exercise right after meals.

## Dog feeding schedule
Adult dogs are usually fed twice a day, about 8 to 12 hours apart. Puppies under six months need three to four small meals a day. Keep feeding times consistent, measure portions, and provide fresh water at all times.

## Dog grooming
Brush short-coated dogs weekly and long-coated dogs (for example Golden Retrievers) several times a week to prevent mats. Bathe every 4 to 8 weeks unless the dog gets dirty. Trim nails every 3 to 4 weeks and check ears weekly.

## Dog vet visits and vaccines
Healthy adult dogs need a wellness exam once a year. Puppies need a vaccine series every 3 to 4 weeks until about 16 weeks. Senior dogs (7 years and older) should be seen every six months. Heartworm and flea and tick prevention are usually given monthly.
