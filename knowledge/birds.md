# Bird care

## Bird feeding
Feed a quality pellet diet with fresh vegetables daily, and seeds only as a treat. Change food and water every day. Never feed avocado, chocolate, caffeine or onions, which are toxic to birds.

## Bird cage cleaning
Replace the cage liner daily and wash food and water dishes daily. Deep clean the cage and perches weekly. Keep the cage away from kitchen fumes, since non-stick cookware fumes can be fatal to birds.

## Bird out-of-cage time and enrichment
Most pet birds need one to three hours of supervised time outside the cage each day, plus toys to chew and forage. Talk and interact with your bird daily.

## Bird sleep and health
Birds need 10 to 12 hours of dark, quiet sleep. Take birds for a yearly check-up with an avian vet, who can also trim wings and nails. Fluffed feathers, sitting on the cage floor or a change in droppings can signal illness and need a vet.
